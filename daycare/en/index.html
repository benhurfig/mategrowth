From Search to Enrollment

  1. 1
    A family finds your center

    They find you on Google, see a Facebook or Instagram ad, or open a link someone shared.

  2. 2
    They explore your website and send a request

    On your center’s website, a family explores your programs and completes Family Request with the care and schedule they need.

  3. 3
    Your team reviews the inquiry and confirms a tour

    In SmartMate Leads, you see what the family needs and agree on a time to visit.

  4. 4
    From the visit to enrollment

    The family visits your center. If they decide to enroll, your team records enrollment in SmartMate.

How we can help

Google Business Profile

Help nearby families find your location.

We organize your center’s Google Maps profile with photos, hours, and contact details so nearby families can find you.

Professional website

Introduce your program before the first visit.

Families can explore your classrooms, hours, and programs and request a tour from their phone.

Family Request

Families take the first step.

Family Request is the inquiry form families use on your center’s website. Parents share their program of interest and schedule. Your team receives the inquiry in SmartMate Leads, reviews availability, and arranges a tour.

SmartMate Leads

From the first inquiry to a scheduled tour.

When a Family Request arrives, your team sees who reached out and who needs a follow-up. Invite the family for a tour, manage the waitlist, or track their next step toward enrollment.

Local SEO

Help parents discover your program in local searches.

Help families find your website with pages about your programs and the communities you serve.

Google Ads & Local Services Ads

Advertise when families are looking for care.

Advertise when families search for child care. We also check Local Services Ads eligibility for your program and area.

Meta Ads

Bring your program to local families’ feeds.

Introduce your program to nearby families with ads on Facebook and Instagram.
