FAQ

A few things to know before we start.

Are SEO and paid ads the same?+

No. SEO focuses on organic presence. Ads use media spend to promote your business. They can complement each other.

Is local SEO just a Google profile?+

No. Your profile is one part. The website, relevant local content and consistent information also contribute to your local presence.

Do you promise results by a fixed date?+

No. Timing and impact depend on the starting point, work performed and external factors. We define deliverables and review, not a guaranteed ranking.
