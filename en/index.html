FAQ

A few things to know before we start.

Do I need to purchase every service?+

No. Start with what you need now. Your scope can include a single service or connect several areas, depending on your goals.

I already have a website or system. Do I have to replace it?+

Not necessarily. We review what you already use. When an existing platform is a good fit, we prioritize setup, integration and training instead of building everything from scratch.

Do you work with larger centers or multiple locations?+

Yes, we can evaluate those projects. Structure, volume, integrations and each location’s needs shape the proposal. Scope and tool capabilities are confirmed before an agreement.

Do you guarantee enrollment or a top position on Google?+

No. Marketing can improve visibility and generate interest, but a family’s decision also depends on location, programs, hours, availability and communication. No specific Google ranking is guaranteed.

How do we get started?+

Tell us about your business. We discuss priorities and prepare a proposal with clear deliverables, responsibilities and terms. Advertising spend, when applicable, is defined separately in the scope.
