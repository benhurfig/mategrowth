FAQ

A few things to know before we start.

Is it just a form?+

No. Family Request is a public entry point. Inside SmartMate Leads, the Request centralizes the inquiry and helps organize next steps.

Is follow-up the same as an abandoned form?+

No. Follow-up is an action to keep a conversation moving. Recovering an incomplete form is one specific use case, separate from the family’s main stage.

Do I have to replace everything I use?+

Not necessarily. We evaluate your routine and existing tools before recommending implementation or integration.
