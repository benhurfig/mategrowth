Privacy

Privacy and your choices.

How this website uses browsing data and how you contact MateGrowth.

Updated October 20, 2026.

Optional analytics

On the corporate pages in this version, Google Analytics 4 loads only after you give permission. It may measure page views, navigation and contact clicks. Your preference is stored in your browser. You can change it in the footer; declining after accepting reloads the page to stop analytics tags.

What our events do not include

Custom events do not include names, form text, phone numbers, email addresses or children’s information. Page URLs sent by our configuration exclude query parameters. A contact click is not recorded as a sent message, confirmed lead or sale.

Business contact form

Your summary is built locally in your browser. This static website does not submit the form to a MateGrowth server or store its fields in local storage. You choose an app and confirm sending there. Do not include information about children or enrolled families.

External services

When you open a project, text message, email, WhatsApp or Instagram, those services’ terms also apply. External links do not change your privacy choices on those websites. Google may process technical information according to its policy and the Analytics account settings.

Specific offer pages

The /gbp-daycare/ and /website-daycare/ pages have been retained from the previous version and use their own implementations. These corporate website preferences should not be interpreted as controlling those pages. The Website for Daycare landing page has its own privacy notice.

Contact MateGrowth

For questions about information you have shared or this notice, email MateGrowthus@gmail.com. Information sent in a business conversation is used to understand your request and provide support.
