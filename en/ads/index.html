FAQ

A few things to know before we start.

Do I need social media management?+

No. Paid campaigns are different from managing your everyday posts. You can continue creating your own organic content.

Can we start without a website?+

Possibly. We assess an appropriate destination, including a dedicated page or form if needed. That setup is scoped separately and does not automatically require a full website.

What happens when a family contacts us?+

Your childcare business reviews the inquiry and follows through. We can connect inquiry management with SmartMate Leads when included in the proposal.
