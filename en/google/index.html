FAQ

A few things to know before we start.

Can you work with my existing profile?+

Yes. We can review and organize what is already there instead of creating a duplicate.

Does my business own the profile?+

Yes. The profile belongs to your business. We use the access required to deliver the service.

Does this include ads or website SEO?+

They are separate services. They can be connected in a project, but each scope must be defined in the proposal.
